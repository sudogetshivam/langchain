Beneath the sun, the field awakes,
A canvas green where silence breaks.
The bowler runs, the crowd holds breath,
Each ball a dance with fate and death.

The willow sings, a fleeting sound,
As leather sails and hearts rebound.
Boundaries blaze like streaks of fire,
Fueling dreams, lifting desire.

The slips await with watchful eyes,
Hope flickers where the moment lies.
A catch, a cheer, the game’s embrace,
Glory etched in time and space.

From dusty lanes to grandest stage,
Cricket binds both youth and age.
Not just a sport, but life’s refrain,
A timeless joy, a sweet domain.
